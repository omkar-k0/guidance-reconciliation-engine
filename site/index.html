Did they hit their own number?

Companies tell investors what revenue to expect next quarter. This engine reads that guidance, finds what was actually reported, and labels each one MET, MISSED, RESTATED or UNVERIFIABLE. Rules handle the clean cases. An LLM only judges the ambiguous rest, and it must quote the filing or its answer is withheld.

How a decision is made

  1. 1
    Extract guidance sentences from the earnings release (8-K, EX-99.1) and parse the range.
  2. 2
    Match the next reported quarter from XBRL company facts. Q4 is derived as full year minus nine months.
  3. 3
    Rule layer labels it and scores its own confidence. Every deduction is written down.
  4. 4
    Gate. Above the threshold, the rule decision stands. Below it, the case goes to the LLM.
  5. 5
    LLM returns a typed decision with exact quotes. Quotes not found in the filing are dropped. No valid quote, no label.

Known limits

  • v1 covers total revenue only, for 11 large-cap US tech companies.
  • “Next quarter” is assumed when the release doesn't name the period; those cases lose confidence and are escalated.
  • A beat counts as MET. The label says whether the floor held, not by how much.
  • Model confidence is not trusted raw. The gate threshold is calibrated on labelled data.
